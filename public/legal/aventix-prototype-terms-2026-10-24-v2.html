1. Demonstration scope

Aventix is being developed as a school project for discovering concerts, gigs, and festivals. This version is offered only for invited evaluation and learning. Some pages show sample content or features that are still being developed.

The demonstration is not an offer to sell admission, a guarantee that an event will take place, or authorization to operate a real event. Do not rely on its sample listings when making travel plans or purchasing services elsewhere.

Participation is voluntary. Declining these terms means you cannot create an account, but you may view publicly accessible pages. Participation does not give the team permission to enroll you in marketing.

2. Accounts and access

Use an email address you control. Choose a password that you do not use for another service, keep it private, and do not share verification or password-reset links. Use only your own account or a demonstration identity that the team has explicitly assigned to you.

Attendee registration creates an attendee account. An organizer application creates a separate organizer account and organization profile. In either case you must verify your email address and then sign in separately. An organizer cannot sign in or use organizer features until an administrator approves the organization. Creating an account does not confirm a booking or issue an admission ticket.

Provide only information needed for the demonstration. A phone number is optional. Do not enter payment-card details, government identification numbers, health information, or another person's personal data.

If you are below 18, involve a parent or guardian and the project supervisor before participating. These terms do not authorize bypassing school rules or any permissions required for participation.

3. Concert organizer applications and responsibilities

If you apply as an organizer, provide a truthful organization name, description, and contact details. Apply only if you are authorized to represent that organization. Contact the project team if submitted details need correction; this prototype does not provide a self-service application edit or resubmission flow.

Email verification proves control of the address, not approval to organize or publish an event. The team may approve or reject an application and may suspend organizer access for misuse or a safety concern. Approval to use a demonstration workspace is not a permit, venue agreement, or authorization to stage a real concert.

For any real event outside this demonstration, the organizer is responsible for obtaining applicable venue, performer, music, image, and other rights and permissions; meeting applicable permit, safety, capacity, accessibility, and age-related requirements; and providing accurate event information. Do not present a proposed lineup, date, venue, price, or availability as confirmed unless you have verified it.

Notify the team promptly if event information changes or an event is cancelled. Do not request or collect payments, sell tickets, or promise admission through this prototype. Real ticketing, cancellation, and refund responsibilities require separate reviewed terms before any sales begin.

Submit only content and personal information you are entitled to provide. Do not enter attendee lists, payment-card details, identity documents, or sensitive information into the application description.

4. Acceptable use

Use the prototype lawfully and respectfully. You must not:

  • Impersonate another person, misuse someone else's email address, or access an account without permission.
  • Attempt to bypass authentication or role restrictions, collect other participants' information, or interfere with the service.
  • Upload malicious content, send spam, harass others, or submit content that infringes another person's rights.
  • Perform load testing, automated scraping, or security testing without specific permission from the project team.
  • Sell, present, or distribute demonstration tickets as genuine admission credentials.

If you discover a security problem, stop the activity and report it privately to the project team without collecting additional personal data or publishing credentials.

5. Events, tickets, payments, and refunds

No real ticket sales or collection of money are authorized under these prototype terms. Any displayed price, seat, booking, payment status, or ticket is demonstration material unless a separate real-service agreement is approved and introduced before a transaction.

A demonstration booking does not reserve real inventory or grant admission to a venue. Do not send money to the team or an organizer based on a demonstration screen.

There is no demonstration purchase to cancel or refund. This does not remove rights concerning an actual payment. If money is requested or transferred unexpectedly, stop and contact the team promptly; do not assume the payment was authorized by this prototype.

Before any real sales begin, Aventix must provide the responsible operator's identity and contact details, the complete price and fees, the event organizer's responsibilities, and the applicable cancellation, postponement, and refund rules. These terms do not establish those future commercial policies.

6. Account information and privacy

Creating an account uses your first and last name, email address, optional phone number, and a password stored as a password hash. Organizer applications also include the organization name and description. The system holds account identifiers, role and status, verification information, application and review records where applicable, and the version and time of your terms acceptance.

Authentication uses session cookies and processes technical information such as IP address, browser information, expiring verification and recovery records, and security activity records. These support account access, email verification, account recovery, abuse prevention, and investigation of problems.

Authorized project maintainers may access information needed to administer the demonstration. The configured database service, currently Supabase, stores application data. When transactional email is enabled, the configured email provider, currently Resend, processes delivery information and the message, including the verification or recovery link. Do not send passwords to the team or by email.

The handling policy is to use this information only to operate, secure, support, and evaluate the demonstration, not for advertising or sale. Use synthetic names for evaluation where practical and an email address you are permitted to use. The team must explain any required use of real names before collecting them.

Before inviting participants, the operator must provide a separate privacy notice identifying the responsible person, a working privacy contact, the basis for processing, where providers process data, and the retention and deletion arrangements, including backups. Accepting these terms does not replace that notice or give blanket consent to unrelated processing.

You may ask the team about your information, request access or correction, or request deletion or restriction where applicable. Account deletion is handled by contacting the team; this document does not promise a self-service deletion feature or automatic erasure when a session expires. You may also seek help from the Philippine National Privacy Commission.

7. Availability and account restrictions

The prototype may be interrupted, changed, reset, or withdrawn during development. Do not use it as the sole place to store information you need. The team should tell invited participants before a planned reset that affects their accounts, where practical.

The team may restrict an account or activity to address misuse, a security concern, or the end of the evaluation. A participant may contact the team to ask about a restriction or stop participating at any time. Stopping use does not itself delete stored information.

8. Content and ownership

Aventix branding and software, and event names, images, and other third-party materials, remain subject to their respective owners' rights. Display within a school prototype does not imply endorsement or transfer ownership.

Submit only material you are entitled to use. If a feature permits submissions, you allow the team to store and display your submission only as needed to operate and evaluate that feature. You retain your rights; these terms do not authorize unrelated commercial reuse.

9. Rights and responsibility

The prototype is provided for evaluation in its current state. The team does not promise uninterrupted service, accurate sample event information, or availability of unfinished features.

Nothing in these terms excludes responsibility that cannot lawfully be excluded, excuses fraud or intentional misconduct, or waives applicable consumer or data-protection rights. These terms do not require you to give up access to regulators, courts, or other remedies available under applicable Philippine law.

10. Acceptance and document versions

Registration links to this document and requires you to select an acceptance checkbox. The system records the version you accepted and the server time of acceptance. Reading this document alone does not record acceptance.

A revised effective document must have a new version and remain distinguishable from the version previously accepted. Changes do not rewrite earlier acceptance records. Future commercial features require appropriate terms before their use; this prototype agreement does not authorize them in advance.

11. Questions and requests

For this invited demonstration, contact the project member who arranged your participation through the same school or direct communication channel used for the invitation. Ask that member to connect you with the project owner for account, privacy, or security requests. Do not send a password, session cookie, or verification token.

This contact arrangement applies to invited participants. A public launch requires a named operator and a functioning published support and privacy contact.
