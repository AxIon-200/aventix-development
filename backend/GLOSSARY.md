# Aventix account identities

Aventix distinguishes account identities by their purpose in the music-event
platform. One person may hold separate identities for different purposes.

## Language

**Personal account profile**:
The name, email address, and optional phone number belonging to one Aventix
account identity, distinct from an organizer's organization profile.
_Avoid_: User profile, admin profile

**Attendee account**:
An Aventix identity for attending concerts, gigs, and music festivals, distinct
from any organizer or administrator identity held by the same person.

**Organizer account**:
An Aventix identity for organizing concerts, gigs, and music festivals, distinct
from any attendee or administrator identity held by the same person.

**Administrator account**:
A dedicated Aventix identity for platform administration, distinct from any
attendee or organizer identity held by the same person.
