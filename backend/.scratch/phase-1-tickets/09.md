## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Enforce explicit per-operation role allowlists with no inheritance, including administrators denied organizer-only routes unless explicitly permitted. Shared self-service permits all three roles subject to access gates.
- [ ] Keep roles fixed at account creation. Different roles require separate normalized emails and independent credentials/verification/sessions; public registration cannot grant organizer/admin roles.
- [ ] Provide local commands to create a dedicated administrator account and disable/re-enable by ID; add no HTTP management endpoints or organizer onboarding.
- [ ] Commands verify the target database, keep passwords out of arguments/logs, preserve genuine consent and verification rules, and persist provisioning/status-change audits.
- [ ] Every protected request checks current server-side status. Disabling invalidates all existing sessions; re-enabling requires fresh login and never revives old sessions, including when no request occurred during disablement. Already-authorized requests may finish.
- [ ] Use isolated organizer/admin fixtures and test-only routes exercising real middleware. Verify all role pairs, immutable roles, disabling/re-enabling, invalidation, trusted-command safety, and that authorization test routes cannot be exposed in production.
