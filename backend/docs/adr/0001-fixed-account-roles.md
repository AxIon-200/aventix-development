# Keep each account in one role for its lifetime

Aventix uses separate accounts for attendee, organizer, and administrator access
rather than converting an account between roles or sharing one account across
roles. This preserves the user's chosen separation of those identities, at the
cost of requiring a person using multiple roles to maintain different email
addresses, credentials, verification, and sessions. The authoritative rules are
in the [Phase 1 roles requirements](../../documents/phases/phase-1-authentication.md#roles).
